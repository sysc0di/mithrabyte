# Data Processing Agreement — template

**Controller:** [name]. **Processor:** [name]. **Subject matter and duration:** [complete].

Annexes must specify: processing instructions and purpose; data subjects and data categories; controller rights; confidentiality; appropriate technical and organisational measures; authorised subprocessors; international transfers; assistance with rights requests and incidents; return/deletion; audits; and contact details. For private/local AI deployments, expressly record the agreed deployment location and whether any external model or telemetry service is enabled. Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review before signature.
