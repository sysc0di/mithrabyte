# MithraByte SEO strategy

## Positioning and intent

MithraByte should be found for organisations evaluating private/local AI, not generic consumer AI claims. Service pages answer commercial investigation intent; product and package pages answer implementation intent; contact CTAs invite a scoped discussion about data, infrastructure, and integrations.

| Locale | Core search themes | Page intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TR | yerel yapay zeka, şirket içi yapay zeka, özel AI asistanı, iş süreçleri otomasyonu, e-ticaret altyapısı, özel yazılım geliştirme | Explain deployment choices, data control, and implementation scope without promising compliance or outcomes. |
| EN | local AI, self-hosted AI, private AI assistant, business AI automation, custom AI integration, e-commerce infrastructure | Reach technical and business buyers evaluating architecture and delivery partners. |
| KU | AI-ya herêmî, AI-ya taybet, alîkarê AI yê karsaziyê, otomasyona karsaziyê, binesaziya e-ticaretê | Use clear, reader-first language; retain familiar technical terminology where Kurdish equivalents are ambiguous. |

## Information architecture

- `/[locale]/services`: primary services hub; link to packages, products, and contact.
- `/[locale]/products/[slug]`: solution-specific technical and business context.
- `/[locale]/packages/[slug]`: implementation and ongoing-support options.
- `/[locale]/legal/*`: transparent policy information, linked from the footer after legal review.

Use one primary subject per page, descriptive H1s, unique title/description pairs, and contextual internal links. Avoid creating thin, near-duplicate keyword landing pages.

## Technical SEO implemented

- Locale-specific canonical URLs and `hreflang` alternates for TR, EN, and KU.
- Locale-aware titles, descriptions, Open Graph metadata, and sitemap entries.
- XML sitemap coverage for service, product, package, contact, and legal URLs; `robots.txt` points crawlers to it.
- Editable product, package, and CMS page SEO title/description fields remain the source of truth for their pages.

## Content guardrails

Do not claim certifications, legal compliance, zero data exposure, model accuracy, performance results, or uptime unless a reviewed contract and evidence support them. Describe local/self-hosted deployment as an architecture option agreed per project, not an absolute guarantee.
